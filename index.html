Miner Journal

Mineshaft

  • Weak points start crit chains. Missing during a chain drops the streak.
  • Auto-crits can jump to nearby rocks when pickaxe tier and forged gear stack enough chance.
  • Large ore bursts are bundled before they fly to the backpack to keep mining smooth.

Backpack

  • Ore that fits is added immediately, then shown as grouped count-up rows.
  • If the backpack is full, overflow is rejected and the full notice is rate-limited.
  • Rare ore value is improved by luck, gear, tavern buffs, and specific forged effects.

Offerings

  • Each ore adds its market value to the offering. Meeting the minimum unlocks the anvil strike.
  • Higher total value reduces junk and pushes more weight into better rarity bands.
  • Higher ore quality matters separately, so rare ore can improve the shape of the chance table.

Forge Rolls

  • Forge skill unlocks stronger tiers, but junk and common stay possible by design.
  • Forge luck and junk reduction make the chance list visibly better before you strike.
  • Forged items can be equipped, stored, sold, or turned in to the trader for upgrades.

Upgrades

  • Pickaxe tiers add yield and auto-crit chain identity.
  • Rare Finder improves rare ore spawning and stacks with luck-based bonuses.
  • Stack Compression multiplies ore stack caps, reaching about 999 stone per stack at max level.

Forge Stock

  • Trader upgrades use forged backpack items as parts instead of coins.
  • Equipped gear is protected from turn-ins.
  • Lower-rarity forged items can be combined into stronger stock when you have enough copies.
